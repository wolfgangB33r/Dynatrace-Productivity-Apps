# 🚀 Dynatrace Productivity Apps

A collection of personal productivity tools for Dynatrace environments — created by me to supercharge my daily work as a Product Manager.

## What's Inside?

These are **custom apps and utilities** designed to make your life easier when testing, debugging, improving, and optimizing Dynatrace observability environments. Whether you're fine-tuning dashboards, troubleshooting data flows, or experimenting with configurations, you'll find tools here to accelerate your workflow.

## 💡 Use Cases

- 🧪 **Testing & Debugging** – Validate configurations and troubleshoot issues faster
- 🔧 **Optimization** – Experiment with settings and measure the impact
- 📊 **Experimentation** – Try new approaches without disrupting production
- ⚡ **Productivity** – Automate repetitive tasks and save valuable time

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

**These apps are provided as-is without warranty or official support.** They represent personal exploration and experimentation during my work at Dynatrace, and **do not constitute official Dynatrace features or products**. Use them at your own discretion in your own environments.

## 📦 Available Apps

| App | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **[Mock Alerts](./MockAlerts/MockAlertsApp.zip)** | Simulate and trigger Dynatrace alerts and problems on-demand. Perfect for testing root-cause detection, validating alert delivery flows, experimenting with alert grouping and denoising, and triggering Dynatrace workflows with mocked alert events. |
